# Obsidian research-vault conventions

## Source material

- Everything under `READ-ONLY/` is source material and the current source of truth for this research.
- It remains provisional research material: preserve its claims faithfully, but distinguish source claims from new synthesis, interpretation, or proposals.
- Do not edit, relocate, or rename source material unless the user explicitly requests it.

## Notes and links

- Create development notes outside `READ-ONLY/`.
- Use standard Obsidian WikiLinks between development notes: `[[Note title]]`, or `[[Note title|display text]]`.
- Never create a WikiLink, Markdown link, embed, or relative link to a file under `READ-ONLY/`, including PDFs, extracted Markdown, and assets.
- When a note needs provenance, identify the source in plain text (for example, “Initial thesis, Chapter 2”) without linking to it.
- Prefer one focused concept per note. Use stable, descriptive titles that become the WikiLink target.

## Research writing

- Separate sourced statements, interpretation, and open questions.
- Preserve terminology from the sources; explicitly define any changed or newly proposed term.
- Use Obsidian callouts for unresolved questions, assumptions, and decisions where helpful.

## Language

- Write every development note in Vietnamese.
- English technical terms may be retained when needed for precision; introduce them with a Vietnamese equivalent on first use.
